EXISTING LIFT MAINTENANCE

Compare lift maintenance contracts.

Four approaches for different building priorities. Start with the operating needs of your building, then confirm the exact contract scope with Gylet.

Based on the four contract descriptions published on Gylet's official maintenance page.

Non-comprehensive

Who it's suited to

Buildings seeking to control the running costs of lift maintenance.

Parts & repairs

Gylet describes a lower-cost maintenance approach, with parts identified for replacement as they approach the end of their useful life. The page does not specify a fixed parts-inclusion list.

Confirm with Gylet

Ask how replacement parts and repairs are quoted, which work is included in the contract, and what is billed separately.

Discuss this option ↗

Comprehensive

Who it's suited to

Buildings looking to reduce the delay involved in obtaining separate quotations for breakdown-related components.

Parts & repairs

Gylet says most components that cause breakdowns and appear on its comprehensive contract list can be replaced immediately without waiting for a separate quotation.

Confirm with Gylet

Ask for the component coverage list, exclusions, repair conditions and any work that still requires separate approval or payment.

Discuss this option ↗

Preventive

Who it's suited to

Buildings where lift downtime is especially disruptive to daily operations.

Parts & repairs

Gylet describes arranging for major components to be ordered and kept at the client's premises to reduce delays when repairs are needed.

Confirm with Gylet

Ask which components are stocked, where they are held, how stock is replenished and what replacement costs apply.

Discuss this option ↗

Customised

Who it's suited to

Buildings whose operating requirements do not fit neatly into the other published package descriptions.

Parts & repairs

Gylet invites building managers to discuss a maintenance package tailored to their needs. Specific parts and repair arrangements are not detailed on the page.

Confirm with Gylet

Ask for a written scope covering maintenance tasks, parts, repair arrangements, exclusions and service commitments.
