# Gylet Elevator Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Gylet Elevator Co Pte Ltd provides lifts, escalators, and mechanical parking systems, along with installation, maintenance, repairs, and upgrades in Singapore. The redesigned homepage helps building owners and facilities teams compare maintenance options and gives customers planning a new lift their own starting point.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make four maintenance contracts easier to compare.** A dedicated section presents the non-comprehensive, comprehensive, preventive, and customised options in consistently structured cards, making each arrangement easier to understand.
2. **Take existing-lift customers straight to the comparison.** The primary hero button, **Maintain an existing lift**, links directly to the maintenance comparison, so visitors can review the options from the first screen.
3. **Give new installation its own enquiry path.** The secondary hero button, **Plan a new lift installation**, leads to a dedicated installation section with a link to Gylet’s contact page.

## Design Improvements

The primary and secondary hero buttons distinguish the two customer paths at a glance. Consistent card layouts and a highlighted comprehensive option give the contract comparison a clear visual hierarchy.
